Over the next decades, what happened to the number of accountants?

When finance work gets cheaper, companies want more of it.

More forecasts. More models. More questions asked of the numbers. Economists call it Jevons paradox.

Today · how AI is sold to finance teams

Same work. Fewer people.

THE USUAL PITCH FOR AI IN FINANCE
Automate the month-end closethe promise
Need fewer finance staffthe payoff

It assumes the amount of finance work stays fixed. In 1979, it didn't.

What most people are getting wrong

AI won't shrink finance teams. It will finally let them do finance.
